## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-11

### Added

- `scripts/duck_music.py`: lowers the music while the voice speaks (`sidechaincompress`) and measures voice versus music level in windows (#25).
- Catalogue of HyperFrames blocks in the skills (transitions, terminal, code) and `docs/style/tokens.css` with the channel colours (#26).
- `scripts/make_captions.py`: SRT subtitles and YouTube chapters from word timings; `check_publishing.py thumb` checks the thumbnail at phone size (#27).
- `scripts/check_render.py`: reviews the render frame by frame per scene with PySceneDetect, RapidOCR and `freezedetect` (expected captions, clipped or small text, frozen stretches) (#28, #31).
- Test plan in `docs/testing.md`: everything that goes into the plugin is tested with real material and a control that must fail (#30).
- Pencil → HyperFrames: `scripts/pencil_to_hyperframes.py`, the `pencil-to-hyperframes` skill and workflow 06, with storyboard mode and scene drafts (`--draft --at`) (#29, #32).
- Thumbnail set on the RAG video after verifying the channel (#19).
- Privacy page for haividio.dev (es/en) and `sitemap.xml`, plus a guide to what Google requires to publish the YouTube OAuth app (#20, #21).

### Changed

- Minimum text size unified at 32, 40 and 28 px depending on the format (#26).
- The star button on the website links to the repository page (#22).

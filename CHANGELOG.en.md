## 0.5.0 — 2026-11-25

### Changed

- `tts`, `check_voice` and `splice_voice` move to the `faceless-video` skill, with the cloned voice guide, and `detect_glitches` to `talking-video`. With PEP 723 dependencies.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-24

### Added

- This changelog, in Spanish and English (#33, #34).
- `scripts/tts.py`: ElevenLabs voice with `language_code`, a pronunciation map and word timings. `speed` does nothing with `eleven_v4`: change the tempo with `atempo` (#35).
- RAG video v2: new voice with pronunciations chosen by ear, `style` 0.3 and ×1.10, a cover at the start, a fade to black between scenes, ducked music and two sentences fixed by splicing. (#37).
- Guides: splice from the silence before the sentence, one YouTube permission per channel, upload subtitles and change the visibility through the API, and `--video-bitrate` when rendering with `delivery` (#37).

### Fixed

- `duck_music.py` failed when the ducked music came out one window shorter than the voice after mp3 encoding (#37).
- `.gitignore`: pronunciation probes, loose takes for splicing and backup copies of word timings stay local.

### Changed

- Skills: each one carries its scripts (with PEP 723 dependencies) and the guides it cites, so they work outside the repository. `scripts/sync_skill_references.py` keeps the copies up to date.
- `faceless-video`: it also activates to duck music under the voice or review an already edited render, and its guide says to stop if a script is missing.
- `memory/preferences.md` becomes `memory/conventions.md`, with general criteria only; personal preferences are kept outside the repository (#38).

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-11

### Added

- `scripts/duck_music.py`: lowers the music while the voice speaks (`sidechaincompress`) and measures voice versus music level in windows (#25).
- Catalogue of HyperFrames blocks in the skills (transitions, terminal, code) and `docs/style/tokens.css` with the channel colours (#26).
- `scripts/make_captions.py`: SRT subtitles and YouTube chapters from word timings; `check_publishing.py thumb` checks the thumbnail at phone size (#27).
- `scripts/check_render.py`: reviews the render frame by frame per scene with PySceneDetect, RapidOCR and `freezedetect` (expected captions, clipped or small text, frozen stretches) (#28, #31).
- Test plan in `docs/testing.md`: everything that goes into the plugin is tested with real material and a control that must fail (#30).
- Pencil → HyperFrames: `scripts/pencil_to_hyperframes.py`, the `pencil-to-hyperframes` skill and workflow 06, with storyboard mode and scene drafts (`--draft --at`) (#29, #32).
- Thumbnail set on the RAG video after verifying the channel (#19).
- Privacy page for haividio.dev (es/en) and `sitemap.xml`, plus a guide to what Google requires to publish the YouTube OAuth app (#20, #21).

### Changed

- Minimum text size unified at 32, 40 and 28 px depending on the format (#26).
- The star button on the website links to the repository page (#22).

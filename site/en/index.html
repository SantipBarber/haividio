What we already learned the hard way

The agent cannot hear

A transcript tells you which words are there, not how they sound. Every delivery comes with a list of minutes a person has to listen to. Voice guide

Approve the take, not the spelling

Three sentences with the same spelling sounded different. The fix was to cut a single approved take and paste it into all of them, without spending credits. splice_voice.py

Search for phrases, not words

Anchoring a caption to a repeated word made it appear 10 seconds early. Search for the exact phrase, and fail if it appears twice. Editing guide

There is not always silence between two words

9 of 99 cuts landed mid-voice and could be heard. Look for the lowest level near the cut and fade 15 ms on each side. detect_glitches.py
